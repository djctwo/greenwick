Greenwick 1.0.0 is the first public release of Greenwick, a free, open-source trading journal for stock and day traders on the Mac. Import your broker's trade history and Greenwick shows you, in dollars, where you make and lose money by session, hour, ticker and weekday.

## Highlights

- **Imports from 10 brokers and platforms:** Thinkorswim, Interactive Brokers, Webull, TradingView, NinjaTrader, Robinhood, Charles Schwab, Fidelity, E*Trade and Tastytrade, plus any other CSV through column mapping. Buys and sells are paired into round-trip trades (FIFO, partial fills, short sales); re-importing skips trades you already have.
- **Built for US equities:** pre-market, regular and after-hours sessions, gaps, sectors and earnings days.
- **Journal, dashboard and 60+ analytics:** notes, tags, emotions, checklists, screenshots, manual entry and replay; equity curve, drawdown, Sharpe and Calmar, Monte Carlo, risk of ruin, opening-range and gap analysis, prop-firm tracker and more.
- **Edge Report:** your top money-makers and money-losers, plus PDF and CSV exports.
- **Private by design:** the journal is encrypted on disk (AES-256-GCM) and stays on your Mac. No account, no subscription, no telemetry.
- **App Lock:** seals the journal key with your password and locks after an idle time you choose (default 15 minutes) and when Greenwick goes to the background.
- **Older-copy check:** an older copy of the journal put back on disk is never opened silently.
- **Password-protected backups** and validated restores.
- **Sample trades:** 30 fictional trades to explore before importing your own.
- **Optional extras with your own keys:** AI recommendations (Anthropic; summary statistics only) and quotes and ticker search (Finnhub; ticker symbols only).

## Install

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Download `Greenwick-macOS-unsigned.zip` below, unzip it and drag Greenwick.app to Applications. You can check the download against `Greenwick-macOS-unsigned.zip.sha256`.

This build is not signed with an Apple Developer ID or notarized, so macOS blocks the first launch. Right-click (or Control-click) Greenwick.app and choose **Open**, then **Open** again. If there is no Open button, try to open it once, then go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. You can also build Greenwick from source (see the README).

A Windows version is coming soon.
