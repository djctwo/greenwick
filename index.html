Features

From broker export to answers

Every feature is available to everyone. Nothing is locked and nothing expires.

01

Imports from 10 brokers and platforms

Drop in a CSV export. Buys and sells are paired into round-trip trades first-in-first-out, including partial fills and short sales, and P&L is shown after commissions and fees. Re-importing a file skips trades you already have.

  • Thinkorswim
  • Interactive Brokers
  • Webull
  • TradingView
  • NinjaTrader
  • Robinhood
  • Charles Schwab
  • Fidelity
  • E*Trade
  • Tastytrade
  • Any CSV, via column mapping

02

The journal

Filters, search, notes, tags, emotions, checklists and screenshots. Add trades by hand, and step through any trade with the replay.

03

Dashboard and 60+ analytics

Equity curve, drawdown, calendar heatmap, win rate, profit factor, expectancy, R-multiples, Sharpe and Calmar ratios, streaks, Monte Carlo and risk of ruin, time-of-day, gap and opening-range analysis, sector exposure, execution quality and edge decay.

04

The Edge Report

Your top money-makers and money-losers by session, hour, ticker and weekday, with the dollar amount on each. Export it as a PDF report or CSV.

05

"What to cut", in dollars

See what is costing you money, and follow a prop-firm challenge with its own tracker on the dashboard.

06

Sample trades to explore first

Load 30 fictional trades from the welcome screen or Settings > Load Sample Trades and try everything before importing your own. Remove Sample Trades deletes only those.

07

Exports, backups and restore

PDF reports and CSV exports, plus journal backup and restore. Backups can be protected with a password, and every backup is checked before it is restored.

A look inside

The main screens

All screenshots use the built-in sample trades. Every dollar amount, percentage and result in them is made up.

Journal

Every trade in one table: symbol, direction, shares, P&L, running total, R-multiple, a letter grade, session and hour.

  • Filter by symbol, session, direction and date range
  • Notes, tags, emotions, checklists and screenshots per trade
  • Manual entry and trade-by-trade replay

Edge Report

Where you make money and where you lose it, side by side, ranked by dollars.

  • By session, hour, ticker and weekday
  • Optional written recommendations with your own Anthropic key
  • Export as PDF or CSV

Analytics

Six tabs: Timing, Sessions, Symbols, Risk, Behavioral and Advanced.

  • P&L calendar and P&L by hour (Eastern Time)
  • Day-of-week, duration and gap analysis
  • Filter by symbol and time range

Import

Drop a CSV export on the window, choose a file, or scan a folder. Greenwick detects the format.

  • 10 broker and platform formats
  • Generic CSV with column mapping for anything else
  • Duplicates are skipped on re-import

Privacy & security

Your journal stays on your Mac, encrypted

There is no account to create, no subscription, no cloud sync and no telemetry. Greenwick works offline.

Encrypted journal

The database is encrypted with AES-256-GCM and is never written to disk unencrypted; it is decrypted into memory only. Its key and your API keys live in the macOS Keychain.

App Lock

Seal the journal key with your password (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 rounds). Greenwick locks when it goes to the background and after a time without keyboard or mouse activity: off, 1, 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes (15 by default).

Older-copy check

Every save is numbered. If the journal file is replaced by an older copy, Greenwick asks before opening it (with App Lock on, only with the password).

Password-protected backups

Backups can be encrypted with a password (AES-256-GCM; on by default when App Lock is on), and every backup is validated before a restore.

Not covered by the encryption: trade screenshots are stored as plain image files, and CSV and PDF exports and backups made without a password are not encrypted. Watchlist symbols, checklist rules and display settings are kept in Greenwick's ordinary preferences file. Read the full privacy page.

Optional extras

Online features are off until you add your own key

Without keys, everything else works fully offline. Both connections use HTTPS with certificate pinning, and each one runs on your own account with that provider.

Anthropic · your API key

AI recommendations in the Edge Report

Add your own Anthropic API key and switch on Allow AI analysis in Settings. Nothing is sent until you click Run Analysis.

Sent
Summary statistics only: overall totals, and P&L by session, hour, ticker and weekday.
Never sent
Individual trades, notes, tags, screenshots or account details.

Finnhub · your free key

Quotes and ticker search

The Browse and Watchlist screens can show quotes and company details and search for tickers, using your own free key from finnhub.io.

Sent
The ticker symbols you look up or watch, and the text you type in ticker search.
Never sent
Your trades, P&L or anything from your journal.

Install

Download and open Greenwick

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Greenwick is free software under the GNU GPL v3.0, and the full source code is on GitHub.

Install the app

  1. Download Greenwick-macOS-unsigned.zip from the latest release.
  2. Unzip it and drag Greenwick.app to your Applications folder.
  3. The build is not signed with an Apple Developer ID or notarized, so macOS blocks the first launch. In Finder, right-click (or Control-click) Greenwick.app → Open, then click Open again. You only do this once.
  4. No Open button? Try to open the app once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the message about Greenwick and click Open Anyway.

Each release also has a .sha256 file, so you can check the download with shasum -a 256 Greenwick-macOS-unsigned.zip.

Build from source

With Xcode 16 or later and XcodeGen you can build Greenwick yourself. The steps are in the README.
